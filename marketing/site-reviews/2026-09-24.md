Review only. Nothing on the site was changed. I checked all 184 pages in the live sitemap, took phone-sized screenshots of the home, contact, blocked drains and Glebe pages, ran speed tests on two pages, tried Search Console, and looked at three Google searches.

## Top 5 ideas (ranked by likely effect on enquiries)

### 1. Fix the home page spilling past the edge of the phone screen
- **What I saw:** On https://www.mrclog.com.au/ in phone-sized tests (iPhone 13, Pixel 7 and iPhone SE sizes), the page measured 570px wide on a 390px-wide screen (500px on a 320px screen). The ☰ menu button and the blue phone icon in the header sat off the right edge. In the screenshot the header showed only the logo. The sticky "Call (02) 9139 8945 — Available 24/7" bar at the bottom was also pushed off the visible screen. No other page I tested has this problem: contact, blocked drains, Glebe, the 24-hour plumber page and the newest blog post all fit.
- **Cause:** A faint decorative blue circle in the home page hero (`src/components/home/HeroSection.tsx`, the `w-[600px] h-[600px]` "Decorative blue circle") is shifted partly off the right side, and the hero section doesn't clip it.
- **Why it matters:** The home page is where most visitors land. On a phone the page can slide sideways or show zoomed out, the menu can't be found, and the always-visible call button can drop out of view. That button is the main way people ring you.
- **Fix:** Add `overflow-hidden` to the hero `<section>`, or hide the circle on small screens. Then re-test at phone width. This is purely visual, with no wording or claims changed.
- **Effort:** small
- **Who:** autopilot website routine. It's a mobile layout fix on one component, which it's allowed to make. The hero file changed on 22 September, and the routine's "don't touch a page changed in the last 28 days" rule may make it skip this. Anthony, if you want it fixed this week, tell the Wednesday run it can go ahead.

### 2. Make the contact page easier to act on from a phone, and settle on one reply time
- **What I saw:** On https://www.mrclog.com.au/contact on a phone, the first screen is a heading and the phone card. The quote form starts about 1,440px down, roughly two full screens of scrolling. The page also says "We reply within 2 hours during business hours" (email card) and "We reply to enquiries within 30 minutes during the day" (above the form). The form's "Type of Job" list doesn't include the two new services added on 23 September (24 hour plumber, toilet cistern replacement).
- **Why it matters:** People who tap "Get a Free Quote" (every quote button on the home page goes to /contact) arrive wanting the form. Making them scroll past it loses some of them. Two different reply times make a customer wonder which one is true. That comes straight after the 21 September work to "settle on one response time".
- **Fix:** Put the form first on phones, or add a "Jump to the quote form" button at the top. Keep one reply-time promise and make it one you can always meet. Add the new services to the job list.
- **Effort:** small
- **Who:** needs Anthony. The contact form and response-time claims are protected.

### 3. Close the reviews gap
- **What I saw:** Our pages show "4.9 Google Rating · 31 Reviews". The competitors ranking for these searches show far more: Mr Splash shows 1,247 Google reviews on https://www.mrsplashplumbing.com.au/plumber-inner-west/, Proximity Plumbing shows 2,576 on https://proximityplumbing.com.au/emergency-plumbing/sydney/, and Network Plumbing shows "800+ Reviews" on https://networkplumbing.com.au/blocked-drain-plumber-sydney/. Our "leave a review" link (g.page) appears on only 2 pages.
- **Why it matters:** Review count is one of the first things a customer compares, and it helps you show up in the Google Maps listings. Our rating is as good as theirs; it's the number of reviews that's small.
- **Fix:** Ask every happy customer for a Google review on the day, using a text with the review link. Even 2–3 a week would noticeably lift the count within a few months. The site can't do this part.
- **Effort:** small (ongoing habit)
- **Who:** needs Anthony. Reviews are owner-only, and the asking happens off the site.

### 4. Link the new suburb pages properly so Google finds and values them
- **What I saw:** The main service areas page https://www.mrclog.com.au/locations (269 words) links to only 16 of the 56 location pages. The 15 suburb pages added on 23 September are each linked from just one or two pages. For example, /locations/dee-why is linked only from /locations/northern-beaches, /locations/blacktown only from /locations/western-sydney, and /locations/sydney-cbd-city only from /locations/sydney-cbd.
- **Why it matters:** Pages with hardly any links pointing to them get found and ranked more slowly. The suburb pages are the ones meant to catch "plumber + suburb" searches.
- **Fix:** On /locations, list every suburb under its region. Where a suburb has blocked drains, hot water or pipe relining pages, link those too. Also link neighbouring suburbs to each other (e.g. Glebe ↔ Newtown ↔ Leichhardt).
- **Effort:** small–medium
- **Who:** autopilot website routine. Internal links are allowed, and /locations wasn't changed in the last 28 days.

### 5. Get Search Console data flowing
- **What I saw:** `node scripts/gsc.mjs 28` ran without errors but returned no queries and no pages at all for 24 August – 20 September. The environment is set to `sc-domain:mrclog.com.au`, a domain property. marketing/README.md says the property is the URL-prefix one, `https://www.mrclog.com.au/`, and that verification file was only added on 23 September.
- **Why it matters:** Without this data, none of the routines can see which searches bring people in, which pages nearly reach page one, or whether last week's changes worked. The monthly check that rolls back changes that hurt also depends on it.
- **Fix:** Check which Search Console property the service account was added to. Set `GSC_SITE` to that exact property, either `https://www.mrclog.com.au/` or `sc-domain:mrclog.com.au`, and confirm the property shows data in Search Console itself. A brand-new property can take a few days to fill.
- **Effort:** small
- **Who:** needs Anthony (account settings).

## Other things worth noting

- **Two pairs of pages chase the same search.** /locations/sydney-cbd ("Plumber Sydney CBD") and /locations/sydney-cbd-city ("Plumber in the Sydney CBD") both target "plumber Sydney CBD". /locations/parramatta and /locations/parramatta-cbd also overlap. The newer CBD page does explain that it covers the city centre proper, which helps. Once search data flows, see whether they're splitting the ranking. If they are, give each page a clearly different focus (the whole inner city vs. apartment towers and heritage buildings). Needs Anthony if it means merging pages, because URLs are protected.
- **Watch the 24 hour vs emergency pages.** /services/24-hour-plumber (new) and /services/emergency-plumbing are close in topic. They already link to each other. Just watch them in search data over the next month.
- **Titles too long for Google.** Most suburb and service-area page titles are 65–85 characters, and Google shows about 60. Examples: /hot-water/parramatta-cbd is 85, /blocked-drains/parramatta-cbd 75, /locations/sydney-cbd-city 79. The ending " | Mr. Clog Plumbing" gets cut off first, so this is a minor issue. Autopilot can shorten them over time.
- **Meta descriptions over 160 characters.** Most /blocked-drains/ suburb pages run 161–181 characters. Some blog posts run longer: /blog/gutters-downpipes-storm-season-checklist 225, /blog/backflow-prevention-testing-explained 209, /blog/why-does-my-drain-smell 201, /blog/toilet-leaking-at-the-base 197. Google will cut these off. Small, autopilot.
- **Home page claims you can't back up.** The home page title says "Sydney's #1 Plumber" and the main heading says "Sydney's Most Trusted Plumbers". Nothing on the site shows evidence for either. That clashes with the site's own honesty rule, and a "#1" claim could draw complaints. Something factual like "Local Sydney Plumbers — 4.9★ on Google, $0 call-out" would be safer. Needs Anthony.
- **Missing "strata/commercial plumbing" service page.** Several suburb page titles promise it (e.g. "Strata Plumbing" on /locations/campsie, /locations/liverpool, /locations/surry-hills and /locations/parramatta-cbd, and "Commercial, Strata & Relining" on /locations/north-sydney), but there's no service page for it to link to. Strata managers are repeat customers. Needs Anthony, since it's a new service page and the wording has to be true.
- **Breadcrumb schema missing** on all 57 /locations/ pages and all 19 /services/ pages. Blog and suburb-service pages have it. This helps Google show a tidy page path in search results. Small, autopilot.
- **Speed (phones).** Google's PageSpeed service refused the request (its daily limit was used up), so I ran the same Lighthouse test Google uses from a cloud computer. Treat these figures as rough: the test went through a network proxy, which makes loading look slower.
