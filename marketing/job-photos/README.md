# Job photos: how to add a job to the website

Photos from real jobs, with a line about what you did, become short
"Recent jobs" stories on the suburb page and the service page. You only
need your phone.

## Three steps

1. **Open the upload folder.** On your phone, open
   **github.com/mrclogplumbing/mrclog-website/tree/main/marketing/job-photos/inbox**
   and tap **Add file > Upload files**.
2. **Pick 1-4 photos from ONE job.** One job per upload, please. If you
   have two jobs, do two uploads.
3. **Say what happened, then commit.** In the **Commit changes** box, write:
   suburb, the service, what was wrong, what you did (and roughly when).
   Example:

   > Paddington - blocked drain - tree roots through old clay pipe under
   > the courtyard, CCTV'd it and relined 9m, no digging

   Tap **Commit changes**.

That's it. It goes live the next morning.

## Good to know

- **Only upload photos you're happy to be public.** Anyone can see them
  on the website.
- **Avoid faces, house numbers, street signs and number plates.** The
  agent will reject or crop them anyway, but it's quicker if they're not
  there.
- **Location data is removed.** Phone photos store where they were taken.
  That's stripped before anything goes on the site.
- **The story only says what you wrote.** Nothing gets added or made to
  sound bigger. If your note is too short to write it up honestly (for
  example, no suburb), the photos wait in the folder and you'll get a
  message asking for the missing details.
- **iPhone photos:** Settings > Camera > Formats > **Most Compatible**
  saves photos as JPEG instead of HEIC. HEIC is converted if possible,
  but JPEG always works.
- **Asked for more details?** Upload the same photos again with a
  fuller note. The newest note is the one used.
- **Sent the wrong photo?** Delete it from the inbox folder before the
  next morning (open the photo, tap the ... menu, Delete file). If it's
  already live, ask Claude to take that story down.
